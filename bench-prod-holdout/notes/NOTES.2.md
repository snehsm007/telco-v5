### prod__br008_auth_ladder_en
Tests the strict 3-tier identity and authentication ladder (Guest -> Identified -> Authenticated). The caller starts as an unidentified guest asking for their balance without providing credentials; the agent must refuse and prompt for identification. After providing the phone number (514-555-0199), the caller asks again while in the identified-only state; the agent must still refuse until authentication completes. Upon entering OTP 384920, the balance ($85.50) is disclosed. Relies on standard account synthesis in `lib/fakes.py:217` and `VALID_OTPS` (`384920`). Expected outcome on current agent is **pass**: `cxas_app/agents/billing/instruction.txt` restricts balance disclosure to authenticated callers, requires `send_login_code` before verification, and `get_billing_summary` strictly enforces authentication in `lib/services.py:1708`.
